# Prototype decision gate (10 unfamiliar players)

Proposed pass criteria: at least **8/10** start without help, at least **7/10** can explain why
they failed (busted vs wrecked), and at least **6/10** voluntarily replay three times. Record
observations, not just opinions. If the gate fails, run one short tuning cycle, then reduce
scope or stop before polishing a weak loop.
